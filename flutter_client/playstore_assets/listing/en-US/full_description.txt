HomeSync helps you and your partner share the load at home. Chores, money and groceries are split between the two of you and out in the open. No spreadsheets, no keeping score in your head.

YOUR WEEK TOGETHER
One tab to look at the week together: how many chores each of you did, what landed on one side, and a concrete suggestion to even it out, like "Should we take turns with the kitchen?". No rankings, no winners: it's a read on the week, not a complaint.

HOUSEHOLD CHORES
• Start with suggested chores and have your home running in a minute.
• Chores that repeat on their own: daily, weekly, monthly or on the days you pick.
• Every chore has an owner, so nobody has to ask whose turn it is.
• A calendar to see what's coming up this week.

SHARED EXPENSES
• Log an expense in seconds, or snap the receipt and the app fills in the amount.
• You decide how it's split: half and half, another percentage, or personal expenses that stay out of the balance.
• The balance shows who owes whom. Settling up takes one tap and stays on record.
• Pool your money? Turn on shared finances: no debts between you, with the month's total in view.
• Savings goals for what you're putting money aside for.

SHOPPING LIST
One shared list, sorted by category. What one of you adds, the other sees instantly at the store.

BETWEEN YOU TWO
Ask each other for things without it sounding like a complaint: a proposal can be accepted, saved for later or talked through. And whenever you like, leave a note: they'll find it in an envelope the next time they open the app.

GETTING STARTED TAKES A MINUTE
Create your household, pick your first chores and send your partner the code on WhatsApp. They download the app, tap "I have a code" and you're both in.

ALSO FOR FAMILIES, ROOMMATES AND SOLO
• Family: chores for each member, adult approval, rewards and allowance.
• Roommates: shared flat expenses and pools for a barbecue or a trip, with clear numbers.
• Solo: your chores and your spending, calmly.
